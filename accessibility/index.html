Accessibility Statement

IAEA Navigator — Last updated May 2026

Our commitment

We are committed to making IAEA Navigator accessible to as many people as possible. We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA guidelines.

Known limitations

We are actively working to improve accessibility. Some areas under active development include colour contrast on secondary UI elements and semantic labels on custom widgets.

Feedback

If you experience accessibility barriers, please contact us at johnhmoore01@gmail.com and we will work to address them promptly.

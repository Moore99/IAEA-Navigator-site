Privacy Policy

IAEA Navigator — Last updated May 2026

What data we collect

When you create an account we collect your email address. We also store your chosen role, bookmarks, and any private notes you add to publications.

How we use it

Your data is used solely to operate the app: to authenticate you, personalise search results by role, and persist your bookmarks and notes across devices. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

Analytics

Aggregated, anonymised search query counts may be reviewed by the developer to improve content coverage. No personal information is associated with these counts.

Data deletion

You may delete your account at any time from within the app. All associated data (bookmarks, notes, profile) is permanently removed within 30 days.

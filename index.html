Frequently asked questions

How do I search for publications?

Use the search bar on the home screen. IAEA Navigator uses semantic search, so you can describe what you're looking for in plain language rather than typing exact keywords.

What does "Explain This" do?

"Explain This" generates a plain-language summary of a publication or section using AI, to help you quickly understand technical content before reading the full document.

How do I save or bookmark a publication?

Tap the bookmark icon on any publication's detail page. Saved publications appear in your Bookmarks tab and sync to your account.

I'm having trouble signing in.

IAEA Navigator supports sign-in with Apple, Google, or email. If you're stuck, email support@kernkraftconsult.com with the device and sign-in method you're using and we'll help you sort it out.

How do I delete my account or data?

You can delete your account from within the app's account settings, or visit our account deletion page for instructions and manual removal requests.
